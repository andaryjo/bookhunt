Contribute a bookshelf photo

You can contribute by uploading photos of a public bookshelf. Our system will automatically analyze the photos and add all the books to the site. For best results, take close-ups of single shelves.

The location gets derived from the photo metada. The EXIF metadata will be removed before uploading and never leaves your device. By uploading you agree to the data being made public under the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license.
