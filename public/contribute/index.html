Contribute a bookshelf photo

New contributions have been disabled and bookhunt.eu is now in a read-only mode. To understand why, read on GitHub.

You can contribute by uploading photos of a public bookshelf. Our system will automatically analyze the photos and add all the books to the site. For best results, take close-ups of single shelves. By uploading you agree to the data being made public under the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license.

Photo Upload on Android & iOS

This site relies on photo location metadata to determine a bookshelf for your photos. But Android & iOS now automatically remove this metadata for privacy reasons when uploading photos. To make it easier for you to find the correct bookshelf, you can use your current location.
